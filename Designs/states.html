§ Design system · Phase 3B /design-system/states

A reusable state library.

Not a component catalogue for its own sake — the states below are the working vocabulary Nayokan uses across every screen. Forms, content, interactive UI, navigation, listings and modals.

Three primary variants.
Five state overlays.

Ink-primary, accent-green, ghost-outline. Each button has default, hover, active, disabled and keyboard-focus states.

§ 09 — Accessibility rules

WCAG 2.2 AA.
No motion-dependent info.

These are non-negotiable rules that apply to every screen. Documented for developer handoff.
