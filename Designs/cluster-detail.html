Join this cluster → See activities
§ 01 — What this cluster is

Priority sector.
Structured collaboration.

Agri-Food is one of Cameroon's most consequential productive sectors. This cluster exists to concentrate skills, tools, mentorship and market access around it.

  • 01

    Shared productive infrastructure

    Members share access to processing equipment, cold-chain logistics and technical tools that no single small enterprise could sustain alone.

  • 02

    Joint market access

    Structured relationships with buyers, distributors and export partners — negotiated at cluster level, not enterprise-by-enterprise.

  • 03

    Continuing mentorship

    Nayokan mentors work with cluster members long after formal VTI training ends. Learning is not a graduation event.

  • 04

    Pathway to capital

    Cluster members with growth traction are eligible for referral into the Startup Centre commercialization pathway and, subsequently, Venture Capital.
