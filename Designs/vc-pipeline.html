§ Venture Capital · Pipeline /venture-capital/pipeline

A working pipeline. No fake numbers.

How Nayokan VC moves ventures from ecosystem sourcing to portfolio support — six stages, honest gates. We do not publish deal-level financials without written consent.

STAGE 01

Sourcing

Ecosystem sourcing from Nayokan VTI, Startup Centre and partner networks. Some external inbound.

Filter: alignment
STAGE 02

Screening

Structured initial screen against Nayokan investment principles.

Filter: alignment + team + market
STAGE 03

Validation

Deep-dive validation of market, technology and unit economics.

Filter: evidence of demand
STAGE 04

Investment Readiness

Financials, cap table, governance and legal preparation with venture team.

Filter: institutional-grade readiness
STAGE 05

Capital

Term sheet, due diligence, close, capital deployed.

Filter: term-sheet fit
STAGE 06

Portfolio Support

Board seat where appropriate. Follow-on capital preparation. Ecosystem support.
