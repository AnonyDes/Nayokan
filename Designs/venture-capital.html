§ 01 — Investment Approach

Three principles.
Long-term capital.

Nayokan VC does not invest in isolation. Capital is deployed alongside training, mentorship, market access and productive-asset development from the wider ecosystem.

Principle 01

Productive capacity, not extraction.

Capital deployed into enterprises that build lasting productive capacity — not into speculation or extraction.

FocusLong-term
Principle 02

Ecosystem alignment.

Priority given to ventures graduating from Nayokan VTI and Startup Centre, or to enterprises operating within our clusters.

SourcingEcosystem
Principle 03

Patient, structured capital.

Structured instruments matched to the venture stage — revenue-based, convertible, equity — designed for productive-sector growth cycles.

InstrumentsFlexible
