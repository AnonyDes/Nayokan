§ Startup Centre · Programme /startup-centre/programme

A structured commercialization programme.

The Startup Centre programme takes validated ideas and research through a six-month, six-milestone commercialization pathway — mentored, milestone-driven, market-tested.

§ 01 — Who it is for

Ventures, researchers,
university teams.

Cohort A

Research-stage teams

University research groups with a validated technical concept ready to move toward a commercial product.

Cohort B

Early-stage ventures

Existing ventures with a working prototype and initial customer traction, seeking structured commercialization.

Cohort C

Corporate innovators

Innovation teams inside Cameroonian corporates commercialising internal projects into standalone ventures.

Cohort D

Independent innovators

Solo founders or small teams outside university and corporate structures with productive-sector ideas.

§ 02 — Programme stages

Six months.
Six milestones.

Each cohort progresses through six milestone reviews. Ventures that meet the milestone continue; ventures that do not are held back or exited with a clear improvement plan.

M1 · Week 1

Onboarding

Venture mapped onto the Nayokan commercialization framework. Milestones agreed.

Mentor pairing
M2 · Week 4

Model & economics

Business model designed, unit economics validated, venture architecture defined.

Model design
M3 · Week 10

Market pilots

First customer pilots or letters of intent secured. Technical feasibility confirmed.

Pilots
M4 · Week 16

Product & distribution

Product build against pilot feedback. Distribution partners engaged.

Product
M5 · Week 22

Investment readiness

Financials, cap table, governance and data room prepared for capital conversations.

Readiness
M6 · Week 26

Handover

Venture moves into Nayokan VC pipeline or continues as Startup Centre alumni.

→ VC · Alumni
§ 03 — Support provided

What the
Startup Centre provides.

01

Mentorship

Weekly 1:1 with a paired mentor. Group critique sessions with cohort peers.

02

Framework & tooling

Access to the Nayokan commercialization framework, templates, and back-office tooling.

03

Market validation

Structured pilot programme with pre-vetted enterprise and public-sector customers.

04

Legal & governance

Corporate structuring support, IP framework, and governance templates.

05

Capital pathway

Priority access to Nayokan VC and co-investor introductions on programme completion.

06

Ecosystem access

Introductions across VTI clusters, hospitality assets and institutional partners.
