§ Design system · Mobile /design-system/mobile

Mobile is not a compromise.

The Nayokan digital ecosystem must remain excellent at 320, 375, 390, 430 and 768px, not only on desktop. Below: breakpoint rules, per-screen framings, and the specific patterns each priority screen uses on small devices.

§ 04 — Mobile-specific rules

The rules
the system follows.

R/01
Hero composition

Photo drops below the headline on mobile. Headline stays large (clamp min 32-40px). Stats grid becomes a 2x2 or single-column stack.

R/02
Nayokan System

Horizontal scroll converts to a vertical stack on mobile. Stage numbering preserved. Progress bar becomes a small step indicator.

R/03
Cards

Multi-column card grids collapse in this order: 4 to 2 to 1 (never 3 to 1 abruptly).

R/04
Forms

Single column. Fields full-width. Labels always visible (never hidden into placeholder). Submit buttons full-width on mobile.

R/05
Tables / data

Non-critical columns hidden at 1000px and below; the essential row identity and primary action remain visible.

R/06
Filters

Chip rows scroll horizontally on mobile if they overflow. Selected state remains visible.

R/07
CTA placement

Primary CTA appears at the top of view AND at the section end. No hunting.

R/08
Image cropping

object-fit: cover with meaningful focal points. No arbitrary letterboxing.

R/09
Touch targets

44x44 minimum. Chips use extra padding. Navigation items are 48+ tall.

R/10
Off-canvas nav

Right-slide panel, full viewport, primary links large + numbered, language pill and CTA at the bottom.
