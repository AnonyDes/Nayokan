§ 01 — Commercialization Pipeline

From idea to
venture — five stages.

Every venture the Startup Centre supports moves through this pipeline. Some ventures enter at earlier stages; others already have validated concepts requiring commercialization.

01

Idea / Research

Original research, academic outputs and technology ideas that show productive potential — sourced from universities and independent innovators.

Universities · Innovators →
02

Model

Business-model design, value proposition, unit economics and initial venture architecture — done with Nayokan mentors.

Mentorship · Framework →
03

Validate

Market validation, pilot customers, technical feasibility and first-revenue evidence — before capital deployment.

Pilots · Customers →
04

Commercialize

Structured go-to-market — the industry-application-focused programme, with partners for distribution and adoption.

Programme · Partners →
05

Scale

Handover into Nayokan Venture Capital for growth funding, or into strategic partnerships for continued expansion.

→ Venture Capital
