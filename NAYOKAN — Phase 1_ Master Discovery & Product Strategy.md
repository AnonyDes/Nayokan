**Project:** Nayokan Digital Ecosystem

**Status:** Working Foundation  
**Prepared for:** Nayokan Website / Digital Platform Project

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nayokan should not be approached as a conventional association website.

Based on the available Nayokan documentation and public ecosystem information, Nayokan is positioned around the development of people, enterprises, innovation, productive capacity and economic systems in Cameroon.

The central strategic idea is:

> **Building people, enterprises and productive systems for Cameroon.**

The digital platform should therefore communicate Nayokan as an umbrella institution that connects multiple capabilities into one ecosystem.

The website should allow a visitor to understand:

1. Who Nayokan is.
2. What Nayokan does.
3. How the different Nayokan divisions connect.
4. Which opportunity is relevant to them.
5. How they can participate.
6. What evidence of impact exists.
7. How they can partner with Nayokan.

The website is therefore not simply an informational website.

It should function as the:

> **Digital front door to the Nayokan ecosystem.**

# 3. CORE POSITIONING

## Working institutional positioning

Nayokan is an umbrella development institution focused on building people, enterprises and productive systems for Cameroon.

Its ecosystem connects:

**Capability → Production → Markets → Innovation → Capital → Productive Assets**

This framework should become one of the major storytelling mechanisms of the digital experience.

# 4. THE NAYOKAN SYSTEM

The website should explain Nayokan as a connected system rather than a collection of unrelated programmes.

## Stage 01 — Build Capability

Develop practical skills, knowledge and entrepreneurial capacity.

## Stage 02 — Organize Production

Help people and enterprises turn capabilities into productive activity.

## Stage 03 — Create Demand

Connect productive capacity to customers, markets and opportunities.

## Stage 04 — Commercialize Innovation

Move ideas, research and innovation toward validated products, ventures and market adoption.

## Stage 05 — Mobilize Capital

Create pathways toward funding, investment readiness and financial resources.

## Stage 06 — Build Productive Assets

Develop or support assets and infrastructure capable of producing long-term economic value.

This model should be visually represented on the website.

# 9. DIGITAL EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

## Principle 01 — Clarity before complexity

The visitor should understand Nayokan quickly.

## Principle 02 — Institution before decoration

Visual design should reinforce credibility.

## Principle 03 — People before stock imagery

Use authentic photography whenever possible.

## Principle 04 — Evidence before exaggerated claims

Impact numbers must be verified.

## Principle 05 — Ecosystem before isolated programmes

Show how programmes fit into the larger Nayokan system.

## Principle 06 — Action should be obvious

Users should always know what they can do next.

## Principle 07 — Mobile-first

The experience must work extremely well on smartphones.

## Principle 08 — Premium but accessible

Premium does not mean complicated.

# 16. CONTENT GOVERNANCE

This is a critical requirement.

Some information currently available in the project materials is identified as requiring confirmation before public publication.

Therefore, the CMS should support a content lifecycle:

**DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → PUBLISHED**

Potentially:

**ARCHIVED**

after publication.

No unverified information should automatically appear on the public website.

This particularly applies to:

- impact statistics
- partner names
- programme dates
- financial information
- investment information
- portfolio information
- properties
- leadership
- legal information
- testimonials
